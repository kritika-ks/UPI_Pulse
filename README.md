# UPI Pulse — UPI Market Share Analytics Dashboard

**Tool used:** Claude AI

In this project, I built an interactive dashboard analyzing transaction-volume share among India's top 10 UPI TPAPs, using NPCI's official monthly statistics. Collaborating with Claude AI on data modeling and visualization design, I tracked a year of market trends, including a dedicated feature monitoring the combined dominance of the two leading TPAPs. 

One interesting insight for June 2026 was that Whatsaap has crossed Cred to become rank-8 in terms of monthly transaction volume while Cred slipped to rank-9.
