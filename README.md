# UPI Pulse — UPI Market Share Analytics Dashboard

**Tools used:** Claude AI

In this project, I built an interactive dashboard analyzing transaction-volume share among India's top 10 UPI payment providers, using NPCI's official monthly statistics. Collaborating with Claude AI on data modeling and visualization design, I tracked a year of market trends, including a dedicated feature monitoring the combined dominance of the two leading providers.

🔗 **View the live dashboard:** [https://kritika-ks.github.io/upi_pulse/](https://kritika-ks.github.io/upi_pulse/)
